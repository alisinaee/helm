# WP30: overview, inbox, activity

- `web/js/views/overview.js` (replaces the WP28 placeholder): project scope shows the limit banner (worst row at 80% or more, with Override for 1 hour), the Running or Stopped banner with one button, KPI tiles, the Inbox preview (up to 5), Agents live (running.js), meters (today, this week and the limit rows), open blockers and the newest 15 activity lines. All projects shows the limit banner across projects, the project cards and Running now (home.js), and an Inbox preview grouped by project.
- `web/js/views/inbox.js`: question cards with one button per option, the default highlighted, a countdown for `timeoutS`, the task link, and needs-you tasks below. Answers use `run()` with `question.answer`; an answered card collapses until the next snapshot. `a` then `1`-`4` answers the focused question (or the first open one). `pageAction()` handles the answer, undo-line and override clicks from app.js.
- `web/js/views/activity.js`: the activity lines, newest first (merged across projects in All projects, 50 at most); Undo where the line says `undo: true` (op `undo`). Source filters stay LATER.
- `web/js/state.js`: the snapshot keys these views read are normalized (`questions`, `needsYou`, `activity`, `blocked`, `issues`, `dayUse`, `weekUse`, and `control.warn`).
- `web/js/routes.js`: Inbox and Activity register their modules.
- `web/js/app.js`: one import and one click branch for `pageAction`.
- `web/css/flow.css`: the question card, the needs-you and limit rows, activity rows, and the Overview grid (one column on phones, two from 1024 px). Token-only.
- Tests: `tests/web/test_sections.py` renders the three views in node (`render_sections.mjs`) from the golden snapshot and a synthetic one, and checks the markup, the empty states, the limit banner threshold, undo placement, escaping, the registry and that every emitted data-action is handled by app.js. `test_shell.py` checks `flow.css` (4 KB or less) instead of the old 400-byte stub.

Known gaps:
- `question.answer` has no inverse on the server, so an answered question cannot be undone and gets no Undo toast. Fixing it needs a Python change (`roadmap/services/commands.py`), which is out of this package.
- The snapshot keeps only the newest 15 activity lines, so Activity shows at most 15 per project.
- The WP28 Phases, Next up, Recent progress, Extras and Legend panels and the usage band were on the old Overview; they now have no page until Roadmap (PE) lands.
