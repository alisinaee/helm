# WP41: rules and definition of done in pickups

- `roadmap/services/hooks.py`: the Stop pickup (`_stop_pickup`) and the SessionStart context now append `rule:` lines
  (rules show for the lead, at most 12) and `done:` lines (the task's `done` rules) for the queued task. Added text is
  redacted (`roadmap.log.redact`, then the hook's secret pattern) and capped at 800 characters.
- Fail-open: the rules are read in a daemon thread, joined for the rest of the 300 ms PreToolUse budget. An error, a
  corrupt rules file, no time left or a timeout omits the rules and still surfaces the request. Logged by type only.
- Unchanged: the 3 pickups per session cap, the per-task-set counter and the control-state counters.
- Tests: `tests/unit/test_hook_pickup_rules.py` (11 tests: lines for a task with rules, nothing without rules, 800
  character cap, redaction, corrupt file, cap with rules, slow injected read, no budget left, SessionStart lines).
