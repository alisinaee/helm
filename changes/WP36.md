# WP36: wide screens and cosmetic fixes

Full width (1280 px cap already removed in 4dac1b8). Smoke now also runs overview, work board, roadmap, tokens and
agents of alpha at 1920x1080 and 2560x1080, dark (tools/ui_smoke.py WIDE_COMBOS / WIDE_LABELS). Screenshots read.

## Problems found and fixed
1. Wide screens: page notes ran past 100 characters on 1920 and 2560. Fix: `.page-note` max-width 90ch (components.css).
2. Agents: Stop wrapped onto its own line on running rows. Fix: elapsed, tokens, blocked badge, handed chip and Stop
   sit in one `.ag-tail` group (agents.js, components.css).
3. Agents: token numbers in "Last 10 finished" were ragged. Fix: tokens are their own right-aligned `.act-tok` column.
4. Tokens: the cap input sat about 9 px above the Save cap button. Fix: `.spend-row` aligns items to the bottom.
5. Tokens: the empty limits block was a large centred message. Fix: a left-aligned `.lim-note` line (limits.js).
6. Roadmap: Approve shown on finished phases. Fix: no Approve button when the phase state is done (roadmap.js).
7. Roadmap: approved phases showed a lowercase "approved" chip. Fix: "Approved".
8. Work at 500 px: each phase filter chip took a row. Fix: chips are at most 48% wide at phone width, so two fit a row.
9. Roadmap at 768 px: lane cards used about half the width. Fix: `.rc` grows to fill its row (plan.css).

## CSS budget
Dead rules removed from base.css (`.app` and `.main` padding, overridden by shell.css), and the duplicate `.run-list`
and `.run-title` rules in components.css merged. Total web/css is 52111 bytes (cap 52224).
